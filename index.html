01 Frame

Black steel, lifted off the ground.

A powder-coated steel chassis stands on eighteen adjustable legs with base plates. 60 cm of air underneath keeps out humidity, termites and monsoon water — and leaves the soil untouched.

  • Clearance 60 cm
  • Footprint 7 × 4 m
02 Skin

Glass to live in, timber to hide in.

Floor-to-ceiling sliding glass opens the living room and bedroom to the garden. The middle module is clad in vertical timber with black X-bracing and a single frosted window for privacy.

03 Roof

Terracotta, the way Bali builds it.

One continuous mono-pitch roof in clay tiles ties all three modules together. It sheds tropical rain to a single front gutter, and deep eaves shade the glass from the afternoon sun.

04 Modules

Built in a workshop. Assembled in a day.

Each module leaves the factory finished — wiring, plumbing, furniture. On site they are craned onto the legs and bolted together, then the roof drops on top.
